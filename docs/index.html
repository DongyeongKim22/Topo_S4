Operator-Level PH Conditioning for State Space Models

Topo_S4 moves persistent-homology-guided conditioning from a scalar forward mask to an additive correction of the SSM kernel itself. A lightweight PH encoder produces a per-sample code z, and per-layer zero-init adapter heads map z to a kernel delta ΔC(x), so every sample gets its own SSM kernel.

  • Diagnosed Three learning-dynamics pathologies: encoder rank collapse, z magnitude shrinkage, C_head rank-1 drift.
  • In progress 200-epoch runs for the current best config (v9b: persistence-weighted pool + orth 5e-4).

Why operator-level?

SSMs are LTI: the kernel is shared across samples within a batch. Per-sample information must enter through either a scalar gain on the output or a modification of the kernel itself. The scalar gain is what our earlier forward-mask paper did; it delivered +0.09pp on sCIFAR-10 for a structural reason, not a hyperparameter one.

Forward mask (prior)

ŷ = H(ω;x) · K(ω) · û(ω;x). A scalar per-sample gain on an already-learned global output scale. Batch-averaged gradients absorb the per-sample variation back into LTI parameters. Expressivity bounded by baseline + scalar reweighting.

Operator-level (ours)

ŷ = (K(ω) + ΔK(ω;x)) · û(ω;x). Adapter parameters do not share a global output scale with the SSM kernel, so per-sample gradient signal survives averaging. No scalar forward mask can reproduce this.

PH as the conditioning signal

Each input's spectrum is summarised by H0 superlevel persistence: stable peaks and their supports become the 4 × 15 feature tensor fed into the PH encoder. Lipschitz-stable under input perturbations (bottleneck theorem).

Method

Per-sample kernel correction

PH features → encoder → code z. Per-layer zero-init head maps z to an additive correction on that layer's output matrix C.

C_eff,ℓ = C_ℓ + ΔC_ℓ(x),   ΔC_ℓ(x) = W_ℓ z

At initialisation W_ℓ = 0, so the model is exactly the S4D baseline. Bias-free heads: no global kernel shift, only per-sample variation.

PH-native persistence-weighted pool

Replaces max-pooling with a parameter-free persistence-weighted sum over peaks. Every valid peak receives gradient, so φ cannot drift into a peak-agnostic mode.

z_d = Σ_p (π_p / Σ_p' π_p') · φ(peak_p)_d

Padding peaks have π = 0 and are excluded automatically. Matches the PH inductive bias: high-persistence peaks are topologically more important.

Scale-invariant orthogonality on W

Penalises shape (singular-value distribution) without pushing |W| up or down, countering the C_head rank-1 drift without suppressing magnitude.

ℒ_orth = &frac1;ℒ Σ_ℓ || (d_in / tr(WTW)) WTW − I ||²_F

Invariant under W → cW. Trained with λ = 5×10-4. A too-strong coefficient (5×10-2) suppresses |W| into the "negligible magnitude" regime; the scale-invariant form fixes this.

Backward-only masking doesn't work

A tempting alternative is identity forward + masked backward. It has no objective function whenever the mask doesn't commute with the Hessian.

M ∇²ℒ(y) = ∇²ℒ(y) MT  ⇔  ˜g conservative

Fails generically. Our forward ΔC formulation has standard backprop and a well-defined loss.

Three learning-dynamics pathologies

Operator-level conditioning is far more sensitive to learning dynamics than the forward mask was. We identified three failure modes in sequence along the pipeline, each diagnosed by a 20-epoch structural audit.

1. Encoder rank collapse

PointNet+DeepSets encoder (φ + max + ρ-MLP + LayerNorm): pairwise cosine at maxpool 0.53, at ρ output 0.98. Final LN strips magnitude, ρ drifts to rank-1 in depth.

Fix: remove final LN, add orthogonal-init residual ρ block. Drove encoder-stage cosine 0.98 → 0.81.

2. z magnitude shrinkage

After the encoder fix: ||z|| falls from ~3.0 at epoch 5 to ~0.4 at epoch 20. Adapter bias inflates 4× to compensate. Per-sample share of the adapter output falls from 86% to 24%.

Fix: remove all post-pool learnable layers. φ outputs d_z directly; pool output is z. Nothing to shrink.

3. C_head rank-1 drift

Even with healthy z, the per-layer W drifts to rank-1: σ1/σ2 ≈ 1.7, effective rank 39/64. Every sample receives kernel correction in the same direction regardless of upstream diversity.

Fix: scale-invariant orthogonality penalty on W with mild λ. Effective rank recovers to 63/64, cosine propagation z 0.76 → C_head 0.79.

This week's progress

This week closed the diagnosis loop for operator-level conditioning. After the bias-off experiment reproduced the z magnitude shrinkage (|z|: 3.07 → 0.47 over 20 epochs, Wz/full: 0.86 → 0.24), I stripped all post-pool learnable layers and made φ output d_z directly (v9). This alone eliminated the z shrinkage pathology and bumped |ΔC|/|C_base| into the VISIBLE regime (0.47 at epoch 20 vs 0.35 in v7). But a new drift surfaced: the per-layer W matrix stayed at σ1/σ2 ≈ 1.7 with effective rank only 39/64—W was collapsing the encoder's diversity into one direction of kernel correction. I added a scale-invariant orthogonality penalty, ℒ_orth = ||(d_in/tr(WTW))WTW − I||2F. A too-strong coefficient (5e-2) pushed W into the NEGLIGIBLE regime and lost 1.56pp of test accuracy; the scale-invariant form with λ=5e-4 hit the right point (eff rank 63/64, C_head cosine propagation Δcos = +0.03 instead of +0.18 in v7). Finally I replaced max-pooling with a parameter-free persistence-weighted pool, so every valid peak receives gradient and φ can't drift to peak-agnostic features. The combined v9b configuration (persistence-weighted + orth 5e-4 + no post-pool layers + no head bias) is the first run where every structural diagnostic lands in its expected regime simultaneously. 200-epoch end-to-end runs on sCIFAR, SC35, and Pathfinder are queuing now; the paper draft has been rewritten around this diagnostic pipeline, keeping the PH-Sobolev theory (Parseval decomposition, variance bound, SNR corollary) in the appendix as retained groundwork.

Interpretation

Kernel > output

Conditioning the SSM operator produces a strictly richer model than any scalar forward mask can. The forward mask's +0.09pp ceiling was structural, not hyperparameter-tuning.

Diagnostics first

At 20 epochs the baseline is only at ~80.7%, so test accuracy alone doesn't separate interventions. Stage-by-stage pairwise cosine and W SVD do.

Scale-invariant penalty

Orthogonality penalties that are not scale-invariant force |W| away from the value the task wants. Trace-normalisation decouples shape and magnitude cleanly.
